Orderly glossary

SKU: stock keeping unit, the identifier of one sellable product variant, for example a shirt in
one size and colour.

Backorder: an order line accepted while the item is out of stock; it ships when new stock arrives.

Fulfilment: picking, packing and shipping an order from one of the warehouses.

RMA: return merchandise authorisation, the approval a customer needs before sending an item back.
Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery.

Split shipment: one order delivered in several parcels because its items come from different
warehouses.
